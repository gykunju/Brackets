# Kenyan Curriculum Content for Brackets

## Overview
This document outlines the comprehensive learning modules added to Brackets, aligned with the Kenyan education system (both CBC and 8-4-4 systems).

## Content Categories

### 1. **Core Curriculum Subjects**

#### Mathematics
- **Grade 1**: Numbers 1-100, counting, basic addition/subtraction
- **Grade 4**: Fractions, equivalence, operations
- **Form 2**: Algebra, equations, problem-solving

#### Science
- **Grade 3**: Plants and Animals, habitats, food chains
- **Grade 6**: Human Body systems, health
- **Form 3**: Chemistry - Acids, Bases, and Salts

#### Languages
- **English Grade 2**: Reading comprehension, letter sounds
- **Kiswahili Grade 5**: Uandishi na Insha (Essay writing)
- **English Form 1**: Poetry analysis, literary devices

#### Social Studies & CRE
- **Grade 4**: Kenyan History, independence, national heroes
- **Grade 6**: CRE - Parables of Jesus

#### Additional Subjects
- **Grade 7**: Geography - Weather and Climate
- **Form 4**: Biology - Genetics and Heredity

---

### 2. **Financial Literacy**

1. **Basic Money Management for Youth**
   - Understanding money, saving, budgeting
   - Needs vs wants, banking basics
   - Duration: 3 weeks

2. **Small Business for Students**
   - Entrepreneurship, market research
   - Pricing, record keeping
   - Duration: 4 weeks

3. **Mobile Money and Digital Banking**
   - M-Pesa, digital payments
   - Online safety, fraud prevention
   - Duration: 2 weeks

---

### 3. **Digital Skills**

1. **Computer Basics for Beginners**
   - Computer parts, typing skills
   - Basic software, internet basics
   - Duration: 4 weeks

2. **Microsoft Office for Students**
   - Word, Excel, PowerPoint
   - School projects, presentations
   - Duration: 6 weeks

3. **Coding for Kids - Scratch Programming**
   - Programming concepts through games
   - Animations, interactive stories
   - Duration: 8 weeks

4. **Internet Safety and Digital Citizenship**
   - Online privacy, cyberbullying
   - Password security, digital footprint
   - Duration: 2 weeks

---

### 4. **Agriculture**

1. **Kitchen Garden Basics**
   - Soil preparation, vegetable selection
   - Planting, watering, harvesting
   - Duration: 12 weeks (full growing season)

2. **Poultry Farming for Beginners**
   - Chicken breeds, housing, feeding
   - Health care, egg production
   - Duration: 8 weeks

3. **Organic Farming Techniques**
   - Composting, organic fertilizers
   - Natural pest control
   - Duration: 6 weeks

4. **Fish Farming Basics**
   - Aquaculture, pond construction
   - Water quality, disease management
   - Duration: 10 weeks

---

## Module Structure

Each module includes:
- **Topics**: Detailed curriculum content
- **Activities**: Hands-on learning experiences
- **Resources**: Materials and tools needed
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
- **Duration**: Recommended time for completion

## Implementation

### To Add Content to Your Database:

```bash
# Copy the SQL file to your database
psql -h your-supabase-host -U postgres -d postgres -f kenyan_curriculum_content.sql

# Or use Supabase SQL Editor:
# 1. Go to Supabase Dashboard → SQL Editor
# 2. Open kenyan_curriculum_content.sql
# 3. Copy all content
# 4. Paste into SQL Editor
# 5. Click "Run"
```

### Accessing Content in the App:

1. **Learning Dashboard** (`/dashboard`)
   - Filter by category: Curriculum, Financial Literacy, Digital Skills, Agriculture
   - View all modules with progress tracking
   - Get AI-powered recommendations

2. **Brackets Page** (`/brackets`)
   - Browse modules by subject
   - Access detailed content and activities

3. **AI Assistant** (`/ai-assistant`)
   - Get personalized study help
   - Quiz support based on curriculum content
